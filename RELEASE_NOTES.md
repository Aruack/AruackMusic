# Aruack Music v1.0.0

### Highlights

- **Premium AMOLED UI**: Jetpack Compose dynamic dark theme with 6 customizable accent colors.
- **Ad-Free Experience**: 100% free of advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, and telemetry.
- **Local Music Playback**: Instant offline indexing and playback for songs, albums, artists, and folder trees via Android `MediaStore`.
- **Jamendo Music Integration**: Direct legal discovery and streaming of Creative Commons tracks via official Jamendo API v3.0.
- **Internet Archive Streaming**: Public domain audio and live archive streaming.
- **Background Playback**: Continuous audio playback with Media3/ExoPlayer and `MediaSessionService`.
- **Media Notification Controls**: Full playback controls on Android notification drawer and lock-screen.
- **Favorites & Playlists**: On-device playlist management and favorites powered by local SQLite (`Room`).
- **Recently Played**: Chronological history tracking stored locally.
- **Serverless Architecture**: Zero backend server, zero cloud accounts, zero middleman proxy.
- **Privacy-Focused Local Data**: All user data, preferences, and listening stats remain on the device.
